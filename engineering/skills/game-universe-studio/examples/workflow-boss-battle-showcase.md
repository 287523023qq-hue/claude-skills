# Example Workflow: Boss Battle Encounter Showcase (Tiger Vanguard)

This end-to-end example demonstrates how to build a complete game encounter asset package using Game Universe Studio.

---

## Step 1: Character Consistency Genesis
**Input**: Tiger Vanguard (虎先锋), anthropomorphic martial arts tiger boss wielding dual broadswords.
**Generated Turnaround Prompt**:
```text
Full body character turnaround sheet of anthropomorphic tiger warrior boss, 4 views on one canvas: front view, 3/4 perspective view, profile side view, rear view. Heavily muscled tiger beast in ancient ornate red and bronze battle armor, jagged scar over left glowing amber eye, wielding dual ancient Chinese broadswords with red cloth tassels. Neutral standing pose, consistent muscular anatomy, clean studio lighting on neutral background, Unreal Engine 5 stylized PBR model sheet --ar 16:9
```

---

## Step 2: Cinematic Intro Cutscene Compiler
**Input**: Boss arena entrance cutscene before player combat starts.
**Compiled 6-Dimension Contract**:
```text
[Anchor]: Low Dutch angle 15mm wide lens, camera placed inside crimson blood pool surface reflecting the temple ceiling.
[Mechanism]: Hydraulic crane booming rapidly upward while tilting down.
[Path & Velocity]: Sudden violent upward acceleration, locking into a brief dramatic pause at tiger's eye level, then pushing forward smoothly.
[Subject Lock]: Locked rigidly onto the tiger warrior's gleaming serrated teeth and snarl as blood drips from his twin broadswords.
[Environment]: Heavy crimson mist parting violently, blood droplets flying past camera lens creating micro water droplets and optical flares.
[Ending Anchor]: Pulls back swiftly into over-the-shoulder third-person combat perspective as boss roars and health bar HUD materializes.
```
