## Step 2: Cinematic Track Intro Compiler
**Compiled 6-Dimension Contract**:
```text
[Anchor]: 3 inches above wet asphalt right behind wide rear slick tires, 20mm ultra-wide lens.
[Mechanism]: FPV chase drone flying at ground level.
[Path & Velocity]: Supersonic forward acceleration following the race car through a sharp hairpin neon banked turn, rolling 30 degrees into drift angle.
[Subject Lock]: Rigidly locked onto the twin glowing cyan rocket exhaust plumes shooting out ignition sparks.
[Environment]: Heavy neon reflections on wet rain tarmac, lens water drops, motion-blurred digital billboards.
[Ending Anchor]: Rocketing upward into high-angle third-person chase camera as the 3-2-1 countdown sequence begins.
```
